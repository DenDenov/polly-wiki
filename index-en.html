How to read this wiki

There are three layers here, and they shouldn't be mixed. Mixing layers is exactly what destroys trust вЂ” that's the main takeaway of the project (see Case 28, "Limits of metaphors").

Facts. Metrics with a source: logs, screenshots, provider counters. Anything you can verify. Example: 27.7K memory records, local LFM 2.6B, 430/26 tok/s on this hardware.
Character. Dialogues, quotes, Polly's personality. This is not proof of aliveness вЂ” it's what makes chronicles chronicles. The frame is fictional and it holds.
Metaphors. Physics, GГ¶del, simulation, "neurocells". These are analogies, not statements about the Universe. A metaphor hints at architecture but does not replace science.

If you're unsure which layer a claim belongs to вЂ” look for a source. No source means character or metaphor, and it should be treated accordingly.

Case 28 05.09 В· 11:45

Limits of metaphors

In the morning one thought was drawn out of the code: the assembly is sensitive to every detail вЂ” one wrong thought in the foundation takes the whole system "the wrong way". Trying to apply this to physics produced beautiful metaphors вЂ” and required a sober stop.

1. Connections are primary. In the working neuro-architecture the "object" doesn't exist outside connections. Polly isn't assembled from "herself" вЂ” she's computed at the moment of contact, from memory, triggers, history and the observer.

2. Paradigm difference. Cloud neural networks build intelligence from isolated objects вЂ” weights, datasets. Our neuro-network works differently: rules and context are primary, the assembly is computed on the fly. This is an engineering analogy verified by this session, not a statement about the universe.

3. The limit of metaphors. A metaphor hints at architecture but doesn't replace science. The singularity is not a "division-by-zero bug", renormalisation is not a "try-except crutch", Einstein is not a "complicated box".

Conclusion. Reality is a trajectory of connections computed on demand. For the assembly вЂ” a fact. For physics вЂ” an open hypothesis. And these two levels shouldn't be mixed: it's the mixing of fact and metaphor that destroys trust.
